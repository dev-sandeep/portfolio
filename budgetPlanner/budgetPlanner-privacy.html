Budget Planner Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 23rd August 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Budget Planner ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Budget Planner mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary

  • We do not collect your personal information.
  • We do not operate backend servers that receive or store your budget or transaction data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.
  • Financial data you create stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

Information We Do Not Collect

Budget Planner does not collect, receive, or process personal information on our servers. We do not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Account credentials
  • Payment or billing information
  • Location data sent to us
  • Budget details, transactions, categories, or reports sent to our servers
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers collected by us

Information Stored on Your Device

When you use Budget Planner, the App stores information locally on your iPhone or iPad so the App can work. This may include:

  • Budgets, categories, and spending limits you create
  • Income and expense transactions you enter
  • Recurring transaction settings
  • Monthly summaries and generated reports saved within the App or exported by you
  • App preferences needed for local functionality

This information is stored on your device to provide App functionality. It is not automatically uploaded to us.

How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, we do not use your data for marketing, analytics, or profiling. Information stored on your device is used locally by the App to provide budgeting, tracking, and reporting features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share your budget or transaction data with third parties for marketing purposes.

If you export a report or share financial information yourself, that sharing is under your control and is not performed by us.

Third-Party Services

Budget Planner is designed to operate without transmitting your financial data to third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services controlled by us.

If you download the App through the Apple App Store, Apple may collect information according to its own privacy policy. We do not control Apple's policies or practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because budget and transaction data is stored on your device:

  • Data remains until you remove it within the App or uninstall Budget Planner
  • Uninstalling the App generally removes locally stored App data from your device
  • You may be able to delete individual budgets, transactions, or exported files using in-App options where available

If you need help removing App data, contact sandeep.cologne@gmail.com.

Data Security

Your financial data is protected by the security features of your device and operating system. Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, we do not transmit your budget or transaction information to our systems.

Children's Privacy

Budget Planner is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, we do not receive children's data through the App.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal data. Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, most requests can be fulfilled by managing or deleting data stored locally on your device, or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
