Frequently Asked Questions

What is Monthly finance planner?

Monthly finance planner is a personal finance app designed to help you set budgets, track income and expenses, and review your spending from your iPhone or iPad. It gives you a structured workflow to plan, record, and understand your money in one place.

How do I create a budget?

Open the app and create a budget for the category or spending area you want to track. Set a limit for the period you are planning, then add transactions as you spend so you can see progress against that budget.

How do I add income or expenses?

Record a new transaction in the app, choose whether it is income or an expense, enter the amount and details, and assign it to a category. This keeps your activity organized and reflected in your budgets and summaries.

How do I use categories?

Categories group related transactions and budgets — for example groceries, rent, or transport. Assign categories when you add transactions so your spending is easier to review and compare over time.

How do I see my monthly overview?

Use the overview or dashboard in the app to see income, expenses, and budget progress for the current month. This gives you a quick picture of whether you are on track.

How do recurring transactions work?

For regular bills, subscriptions, or income, you can set up recurring entries in the app where that feature is available. This helps reduce manual entry for items that repeat on a schedule.

How do I view reports or summaries?

Use the reports or summary section in the app to review spending by category, budget, or time period. This can help you understand patterns and plan ahead.

How do I contact support?

Email us at mail.sandlabs@gmail.com with a description of your question or issue. Including your app version and device model helps us respond faster.

Does Monthly finance planner collect my data?

No. Monthly finance planner does not collect your personal information or upload your financial data to our servers. Budgets, transactions, and summaries stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

How do I delete my budget data?

You can delete individual budgets or transactions within the App where that option is available. Uninstalling Monthly finance planner from your device generally removes locally stored App data. If you need help, contact mail.sandlabs@gmail.com.

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of Monthly finance planner from the App Store.
  • Restart the app if you encounter an unexpected issue.
  • Check your device storage if the App has trouble saving data locally.
  • Contact support with the app version, device model, and a clear description of the problem.
