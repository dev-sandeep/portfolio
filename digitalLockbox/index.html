Digital Lockbox is a secure document storage app designed to make it easier to keep important files in one organized place. Whether you are storing receipts, IDs, contracts, insurance documents, or personal records, Digital Lockbox gives you a structured way to add, organize, and access documents from your iPhone or iPad — without uploading them to our servers.

Built for secure document management

From storing and organizing to searching and viewing — keep your important files in one workflow on your device.

Store documents on your device

Add documents and files to Digital Lockbox so they are kept in one dedicated place instead of scattered across your camera roll, downloads, or messaging apps.

Files you add stay on your iPhone or iPad and are not automatically uploaded to developer servers.

Organize with folders

Group related documents into folders — such as personal records, travel, insurance, or work — so you can browse and manage items by category.

A clear folder structure makes it easier to maintain your document library over time.

Find documents quickly

Use search to locate documents when you need them. Instead of scrolling through unrelated files on your device, you can go straight to what you stored in Digital Lockbox.

View documents in the app

Open and review stored documents within Digital Lockbox so you can access important information when you need it — whether at home or on the go.

Add from files or photos

Import documents from sources available on your device, such as the Files app or photo library, where those options are supported in the App.

This helps you bring existing documents into your lockbox without starting from scratch.

Keep your vault organized

Digital Lockbox provides a focused workspace for your stored items. See what you have saved, manage folders, and keep your document collection tidy.

Protected by your device

Your stored documents are protected by the security features of your device and operating system, such as device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID where you use them.

Because we do not collect your files on our servers, your document library remains under your control on your device.

Designed for iPhone and iPad

Digital Lockbox is designed for use on iPhone and iPad, so you can store and access documents when it suits you.

On iPad, the larger screen provides more room to browse folders, preview documents, and manage your library.

Who is Digital Lockbox for?

Digital Lockbox can be useful for anyone who wants important documents in one secure, organized place.

  • Individuals Store personal records, receipts, and IDs.
  • Travelers Keep tickets, bookings, and travel documents handy.
  • Freelancers Organize contracts and work-related files.
  • Households Manage insurance, warranties, and household documents.
  • Students Store certificates, forms, and important paperwork.
  • Anyone who values privacy Keep files on device without cloud upload by the developer.

Why Digital Lockbox?

Important documents often end up spread across email attachments, photo libraries, downloads, and paper files.

Digital Lockbox brings these items into a single workflow: Add → Organize → Find → Access. The result is a clearer document library, faster access, and files that stay on your device under your control.
