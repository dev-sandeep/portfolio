Frequently Asked Questions

What is Digital Lockbox?

Digital Lockbox is a secure document storage app designed to help you store, organize, and access important files on your iPhone or iPad. It gives you a structured way to keep documents in one place on your device.

How do I add a document?

Tap Add Item, choose Document (or a related type such as Driving License or Tax ID), then enter details like document type and number. On iPhone, you can use the camera to scan a card or ID and fill fields automatically. Everything is encrypted and stored only on your device.

How do I organize documents with folders?

Digital Lockbox does not use custom folders. Items are organized by category — for example Document, Driving License, Tax ID, Insurance, or Credit Card. Open a category from Home or the sidebar to browse that group.

How do I find a stored document?

Use the search bar (“Search your vault”) or open the relevant category. Search matches titles and item details stored in your vault.

How do I view a document?

Tap an item in your vault to open its details. You can review fields, copy values, and edit when needed. Document entries store structured information (such as type and number), not a separate PDF file browser.

How do I delete a document?

Open the item, then use the delete option and confirm. The item is permanently removed from the encrypted vault on this device.

How do I contact support?

Email us at mail.sandlabs@gmail.com with a description of your question or issue. Including your app version and device model helps us respond faster.

Does Digital Lockbox collect my data?

No. Digital Lockbox is zero-knowledge and offline-first. We do not collect your vault contents or upload them to our servers. Your data is encrypted on your device. Optional encrypted backups are created only when you export them yourself (for example to Files or iCloud Drive).

How do I delete all my lockbox data?

Delete individual items in the app, or uninstall Digital Lockbox to remove locally stored vault data from that device. Encrypted backup files you previously saved outside the app (for example in Files or iCloud Drive) are not removed by uninstalling — delete those files separately if you no longer need them. For help, contact mail.sandlabs@gmail.com.

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of Digital Lockbox from the App Store.
  • If the vault will not unlock, confirm you are using the correct master password, or your recovery key if you set one up.
  • Restart the app if you encounter an unexpected issue.
  • If Face ID / Touch ID fails, unlock with your master password, then check Unlock settings in the app.
  • If scanning does not work, allow camera access for Digital Lockbox in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
  • If backup or restore fails, confirm you are using a valid .dvault file and the same master password or recovery key used when the backup was created.
  • Contact support with the app version, device model, and a clear description of the problem.
