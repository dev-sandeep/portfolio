Inspectly is a professional inspection and field documentation app designed to make inspections faster, more organized, and easier to document. Whether you are inspecting a property, equipment, facility, vehicle, construction site, or carrying out routine maintenance checks, Inspectly gives you a structured workflow to plan, perform, document, and complete inspections from your iPhone or iPad.

Built for real inspection workflows

From planning and checklists to evidence, reports, and history — everything stays in one structured workflow.

Create and manage inspections

Start an inspection by selecting the relevant customer, property, asset, or inspection type. Organize your work into clearly defined sections and inspection items so you always know what needs to be checked.

Inspectly provides a structured workspace where you can see your inspections, their status, upcoming work, and completed inspection history.

Build detailed inspection checklists

Create inspection templates and checklists that match your specific workflow. Break an inspection into sections and individual checkpoints. Each item can be reviewed during the inspection and marked according to its condition or result.

This helps ensure that important inspection points aren't forgotten and provides consistency when the same type of inspection needs to be performed repeatedly.

Capture findings and evidence

An inspection is more useful when you can clearly document what you find. Inspectly lets you record relevant information directly against inspection items, including photos and notes, allowing you to create a clear record of the condition observed during the inspection.

Photos and notes stay associated with the inspection so that the evidence can be reviewed later instead of being scattered across your device.

Track inspection progress

Inspectly provides a clear overview of inspection progress while you work. You can quickly see which sections or items have been completed and which still require attention.

This makes it easier to manage longer inspections and reduces the possibility of accidentally leaving important items unfinished.

Record issues and observations

When you identify a problem during an inspection, Inspectly helps you document the finding in context. Record the observation, attach supporting evidence, and keep the information together with the inspection.

This creates a more complete record that can be reviewed after the inspection has been completed.

Keep inspections organized

Inspectly is designed to keep your inspection history organized in one place. Instead of relying on paper forms, spreadsheets, photographs in your camera roll, and separate notes, your inspection information can be maintained within a structured workflow.

Quickly access previous inspections and review what was recorded.

Manage scheduled inspections

Use the inspection calendar and reminders to keep track of upcoming work. See scheduled inspections at a glance and organize your workload around upcoming appointments and field activities.

Professional inspection reports

Once an inspection is complete, the collected information can be turned into a professional inspection report. Reports can bring together:

  • Inspection details
  • Checklist results
  • Findings
  • Notes
  • Photographic evidence
  • Completion information
  • Signatures

This makes it easier to communicate inspection results with customers, colleagues, contractors, or other stakeholders.

Capture signatures

For workflows that require acknowledgement or approval, Inspectly supports capturing signatures as part of the inspection process. The signature can become part of the completed inspection documentation, helping create a more complete record of the work performed.

Designed for iPhone and iPad

Inspectly is designed for field use on iPhone and iPad, giving inspectors and field professionals access to their inspection workflow while they're working on-site.

The iPad experience provides a larger workspace for viewing inspection details, navigating checklists, reviewing findings, and managing multiple pieces of information at once.

Who can use Inspectly?

Inspectly can be useful for a wide range of inspection and field-work scenarios.

  • Property inspectors Document property conditions and findings.
  • Facility managers Perform routine facility inspections.
  • Maintenance teams Document equipment and maintenance checks.
  • Field technicians Record service and inspection activities.
  • Contractors Document project progress and completed work.
  • Equipment inspectors Maintain structured inspection records.
  • Construction teams Document site observations and issues.
  • Businesses Standardize recurring inspection processes.

Why Inspectly?

Traditional inspections often involve a combination of paper checklists, camera photos, handwritten notes, spreadsheets, and manually created reports.

Inspectly brings these activities into a single workflow: Plan → Inspect → Capture → Document → Report. The result is a more consistent inspection process, better documentation, and less time spent organizing information after the inspection.
