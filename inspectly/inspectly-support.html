Frequently Asked Questions

What is Inspectly?

Inspectly is a professional inspection and field documentation app designed to make inspections faster, more organized, and easier to document. It gives you a structured workflow to plan, perform, document, and complete inspections from your iPhone or iPad — whether you are inspecting a property, equipment, facility, vehicle, construction site, or carrying out routine maintenance checks.

How do I create an inspection?

Start an inspection by selecting the relevant customer, property, asset, or inspection type in the app. Organize your work into sections and inspection items, then begin completing the checklist from your structured inspection workspace.

How do I complete an inspection checklist?

Work through each section and checkpoint in the inspection. Review and mark items according to their condition or result, track your progress as you go, and add notes or evidence where needed until the inspection is complete.

How do I add notes or photos?

While completing an inspection, add notes and photos directly against inspection items or findings. This keeps evidence associated with the inspection instead of scattered across your device.

How do I view completed inspections?

Completed inspections are kept in your inspection history within the app. You can access previous inspections and review what was recorded, including checklist results, findings, notes, and supporting evidence.

How do I generate a report?

After completing an inspection, use the report feature to turn the collected information into a professional inspection report. Reports can include inspection details, checklist results, findings, notes, photos, completion information, and signatures.

How do I schedule inspections or use reminders?

Use the inspection calendar and reminders to keep track of upcoming work. Scheduled inspections can be viewed at a glance so you can organize appointments and field activities.

How do I contact support?

Email us at mail.sandlabs@gmail.com with a description of your question or issue. Including your app version and device model helps us respond faster.

Does Inspectly collect my data?

No. Inspectly does not collect your personal information or upload your inspection data to our servers. Inspection details, photos, notes, and reports stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

How do I delete my inspection data?

You can delete individual inspections within the App where that option is available. Uninstalling Inspectly from your device generally removes locally stored App data. If you need help, contact mail.sandlabs@gmail.com.

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of Inspectly from the App Store.
  • Restart the app if you encounter an unexpected issue.
  • Check your device storage and app permissions where photos, files, or notifications are required.
  • Contact support with the app version, device model, and a clear description of the problem.
