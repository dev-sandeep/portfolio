Inspectly Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Inspectly ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Inspectly mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary

  • We do not collect your personal information.
  • We do not operate backend servers that receive or store your inspection data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.
  • Inspection data you create stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

Information We Do Not Collect

Inspectly does not collect, receive, or process personal information on our servers. We do not collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Account credentials
  • Payment or billing information
  • Location data sent to us
  • Inspection content, photos, notes, or reports sent to our servers
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers collected by us

Information Stored on Your Device

When you use Inspectly, the App stores information locally on your iPhone or iPad so the App can work. This may include:

  • Inspection details, checklist responses, and completion status
  • Notes, findings, and observations you enter
  • Photos and signatures you attach to inspections
  • Inspection history, reminders, and calendar entries
  • Generated reports saved within the App or exported by you

This information is stored on your device to provide App functionality. It is not automatically uploaded to us.

Photos and Media

If you add photos or other media to an inspection, the App accesses media you capture or select for documentation purposes. Photos and media remain associated with your inspection data on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Device Permissions

Inspectly may request device permissions needed for its features, such as access to the camera, photo library, notifications, or file storage. These permissions are used only to provide App features on your device and are not used to collect personal information for us.

How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, we do not use your data for marketing, analytics, or profiling. Information stored on your device is used locally by the App to provide inspection, documentation, and reporting features.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not share your inspection data with third parties for marketing purposes.

If you export a report or share inspection information yourself, that sharing is under your control and is not performed by us.

Third-Party Services

Inspectly is designed to operate without transmitting your inspection data to third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services controlled by us.

If you download the App through the Apple App Store, Apple may collect information according to its own privacy policy. We do not control Apple's policies or practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because inspection data is stored on your device:

  • Data remains until you remove it within the App or uninstall Inspectly
  • Uninstalling the App generally removes locally stored App data from your device
  • You may be able to delete individual inspections or exported files using in-App options where available

If you need help removing App data, contact support@[MY-DOMAIN].

Data Security

Your inspection data is protected by the security features of your device and operating system. Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, we do not transmit your inspection information to our systems.

Children's Privacy

Inspectly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not collect personal information on our servers, we do not receive children's data through the App.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal data. Since we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, most requests can be fulfilled by managing or deleting data stored locally on your device, or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
